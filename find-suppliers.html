What would help you choose?

Share what you know now. We can work through the gaps together.

  1. What you need. The product or service, the job it must do, and any acceptable alternatives.
  2. How much. Quantity, recurring demand or the size of the project. An estimate is fine.
  3. Where. The delivery country and city or project area, plus any limits on supplier location.
  4. When. Your delivery or start date, and whether it is flexible.
  5. What matters most. Required standards, materials or experience. A budget range helps when you have one.

A few sentences can be enough.

Illustrative examples, not live projects or customer results.

PRODUCT EXAMPLE

Paper bags for a retailer

“We need around 5,000 unprinted kraft-paper bags, in three sizes, delivered to Budapest. We can consider European producers. The sizes and delivery date are still to be confirmed.”

Useful next questions: paper weight, handle type, required certification, minimum order per size and sample terms.

SERVICE EXAMPLE

A scheduling system for a field team

“We need a partner to set up a maintenance scheduling system for 20 technicians in the UAE, including data import, mobile access and training. English support is required.”

Useful next questions: current software, start date, security requirements, data hosting and relevant references.
