Describe what you need ↗Here to supply?

Five details make a useful brief.

  1. Product or service: what must be supplied, the specification or scope, and any acceptable alternatives.
  2. Quantity or size of the job: a trial order, monthly demand, number of sites or a defined piece of work. An estimate is useful; say when it is uncertain.
  3. Delivery location: the country and city or project area. Mention any restrictions on where the supplier may be based.
  4. Timing: the required delivery or start date, and whether it is fixed or flexible.
  5. Requirements: mandatory certifications, standards, materials or experience. Separate must-haves from preferences and include a budget range if you can.

Add these details to the form’s “What do you need?” field. A rough outline is welcome; we can work through the open questions together.

PRODUCT EXAMPLE / PACKAGING

Unprinted paper bags

We are exploring an initial order of 5,000 brown kraft-paper bags with twisted handles, delivered to Budapest, Hungary, in February 2027. We can consider European producers.

We need 80–100 g/m² paper and three sizes, to be confirmed. FSC certification is preferred; please state what documentation is available. We need the minimum order per size and sample terms before choosing.

SERVICE EXAMPLE / TECHNOLOGY

A maintenance scheduling system

We need a partner to configure a scheduling system for 20 field technicians in the UAE. The scope includes data import, mobile access and training, with a target start in March 2027.

English support and relevant implementation references are required. Certification, security and data-hosting requirements still need to be agreed before selecting a provider.
