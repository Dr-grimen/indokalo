Prepare your buying brief ↗

From a first conversation to a useful introduction.

  1. Confirm the need and decision-maker. We establish what is being bought or offered, who owns the decision and the next useful action.
  2. Check a focused set of potential counterparts. Compare capabilities, geography, capacity and interest against the same brief.
  3. Make the information comparable. Record what a supplier actually confirms about scope, price basis, minimum order, delivery and documents, and mark the open questions.
  4. Agree terms and the introduction. Both parties choose whether to proceed. The buyer and supplier negotiate and sign their own commercial contract.

I’ll be clear when a company is a research candidate, when it has expressed interest and when something is still unconfirmed. Contract value is not a promise of an order or commission.
