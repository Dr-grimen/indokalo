ACQUIRERS & MERGER ENQUIRIES

What would make a good fit?

Describe your business objective, target sectors and countries, and who is responsible for the decision. Say whether you already work with an adviser.

This is a general enquiry. Offers, valuations, financing evidence and transaction documents belong in an agreed professional process.

Agree the process before approaching anyone.

  1. Clarify the objective: an initial discussion to understand your role, plans and the help you are seeking.
  2. Establish professional support: assess a suitable transaction adviser and the permissions required for the proposed work and countries.
  3. Set authority, confidentiality and fees: agree who may act, what can be shared, who pays and the conditions in writing.
  4. Let the appointed adviser manage the next step: an approved anonymous summary, qualified counterpart discussions and any confidentiality agreement before sensitive information is released.

Your enquiry is stored privately. It will not become a public sale listing or be forwarded to prospective buyers without an agreed next step.

The first conversation is free.

Any introduction fee, who pays it and the work covered must be agreed separately in writing before an introduction. An adviser’s services have their own terms.

Indokalo is not presenting itself as a licensed investment firm or an appointed transaction adviser. This form does not accept bids, securities orders or mandates, and does not commit either side to a transaction.
