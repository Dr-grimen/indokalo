A short introduction is enough to begin.

A sentence about your offer and a link to your products or services make a useful starting point. These details help as we explore the fit:

  1. Your offer. The products or work you deliver, and whether you manufacture, distribute or subcontract.
  2. Your reach. Countries served, delivery restrictions and the languages your team supports.
  3. Your capacity. Suitable order or project size, minimum order, current availability and lead time.
  4. Your evidence. Relevant specifications, current certifications and references you have permission to share.
  5. Your terms. Whether you are open to a referral agreement and who can approve it.

Be clear about what is confirmed and what needs checking. Capacity and lead times should reflect your current position.
