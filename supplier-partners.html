Share your capabilities ↗Looking for a supplier?

Make your introduction specific.

  1. Actual products or services: name the product lines or work you deliver. Say whether you manufacture, distribute or subcontract, and link to relevant public product information.
  2. Countries served: where you can supply or work today, any delivery restrictions and the languages your team can support.
  3. Capacity and timing: minimum order, order size or project scope you can handle, current availability and typical lead time. Include the date of the information.
  4. Evidence of fit: relevant specifications, certification scope and validity, and references you have permission to share. Tell me what still needs checking.
  5. Cooperation: whether you are open to a written referral agreement, who can approve it, and any restrictions on the introductions you accept.

Use the form’s “What can your company supply?” field. A short summary and a link to your products or services are a useful place to start.
