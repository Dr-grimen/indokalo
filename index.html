How I get paid

There’s no upfront fee. Buyers don’t pay me for an introduction. The supplier pays an agreed commission if a deal results, under the written terms agreed before the introduction.

Sending this form doesn’t create a mandate, a supplier agreement or a commitment to pay.

A few details

Before we talk.

Do I need to have won a contract?

No. You may have an active project, an upcoming purchase or a need for a business partner. Explain the situation and what you’re looking for.

Which countries and sectors do you work with?

You can send a request from any country and sector. I review the specific need and whether a practical, suitable introduction is possible. I don’t claim an established supplier network in every market.

Will you share my details straight away?

No. I’ll discuss the request with you before sharing your contact or company details with a potential counterpart. Both sides need to be interested in the introduction.

What does a supplier pay?

Any introduction fee and the conditions for payment are agreed in writing before an introduction. There is no upfront fee or fee simply for submitting this form.

Received an email from me?

You can reply directly with what you need, or use the form above. If you don’t want further outreach, reply STOP to the email or write to connect@indokalo.com.
