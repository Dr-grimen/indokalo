Indokalo

I connect companies that need something with companies that can deliver it.

I'm Sondre Grimen, based in Fitjar on the west coast of Norway. I follow public contract awards across Europe and beyond. When a company wins a contract, I ask whether they need suppliers or subcontractors for the job, and if they do, I look for companies that can deliver.

I also help Norwegian suppliers to the fish-farming industry find customers abroad. I have a trade certificate in aquaculture.

How it works

  1. You tell me what you need for the job.
  2. I find companies that can supply it and check that they are a good fit.
  3. Everything is agreed in writing before I introduce anyone.

About my e-mails

I only write to the addresses companies publish themselves for enquiries, such as info@ or post@, never to personal addresses. If you don't want to hear from me, reply STOP to any e-mail and you won't get another one.
