How I work

More clarity.
A useful introduction.

A company name is only a starting point. The work is checking whether the fit makes sense.

  1. 01

    Understand what matters.

    We clarify your goal, the requirements and who you need to reach. A rough outline is enough to begin.

  2. 02

    Check the possible fit.

    I research relevant companies and work to confirm their capabilities and interest. You’ll know what is confirmed and what still needs checking.

  3. 03

    Put both sides in the conversation.

    We agree the next step, permission to share details and any fees first. An introduction follows when both sides want to talk.

What happens after I get in touch?

I read your enquiry and reply to the email address you provide. We clarify what you need and whether I can help. Sending a request doesn’t create an introduction, a mandate or a commitment to pay.

Which countries and sectors do you work with?

Enquiries are welcome from any country and sector. I assess each one against the requirements and available companies. The reach of a search depends on the particular request.

Will you share my details straight away?

No. We discuss what can be shared and with whom first. For a business sale, acquisition or merger, we also agree the adviser’s role and confidentiality before approaching anyone.

How do the fees work?

For commercial introductions, buyers don’t pay me and there’s no upfront fee. A supplier pays commission if a deal results, under written terms agreed before the introduction. Business sale, acquisition and merger work requires a separate agreement covering the scope, adviser and any fees.
